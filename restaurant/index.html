Our Story Est. 2019 · Ybor City

Ember & Oak was born from a simple conviction: that the best restaurants in the world trace their excellence back to the soil. We source directly from over a dozen Florida farms within 150 miles — produce picked at peak, delivered the same morning it hits your plate.

Chef Mateo Rivera trained in San Sebastián before returning to his Tampa roots. He brought the discipline of European technique and applied it to the flavors he grew up with — the citrus, the Gulf seafood, the spice of Ybor City. The result is a menu that feels both refined and deeply personal.

At the heart of our kitchen burns a live white oak fire. Every dish passes through its heat — whether it's a slow roast, a quick char, or a gentle smoke. The fire is not a gimmick. It is the kitchen.
