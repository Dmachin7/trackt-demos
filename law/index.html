Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions? Call us at (813) 555-0192 or submit the form above — we're happy to help.

How much does it cost to hire Meridian Legal Group?
For most personal injury and employment cases, we work on a contingency fee basis — meaning you pay nothing upfront and our fee is a percentage of the amount we recover for you. If we don't win, you owe us nothing. For business litigation and family law matters, we offer flat-rate and hourly arrangements depending on the scope. We discuss fees clearly at your free consultation so there are never any surprises.
How long will my case take?
Case timelines vary widely. Many personal injury cases settle within 6–18 months. Complex business litigation can take 2–3 years if the case proceeds to trial. Family law matters involving custody depend heavily on whether parties can agree or whether full litigation is required. We'll give you an honest timeline estimate at your consultation based on the specifics of your situation, and keep you updated at every stage.
What should I bring to my consultation?
Bring whatever you have — don't worry if it's not organized. Helpful documents include any police reports, medical records, contracts, emails, photographs, insurance correspondence, employment documents, or court paperwork related to your situation. If you have nothing on hand yet, that's completely fine. The consultation is about understanding your case, and our team can help gather additional information afterward.
Do you handle cases outside of Tampa?
Yes. While our home is Tampa, we are licensed to practice throughout the state of Florida and regularly handle cases in Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Sarasota, and Polk counties. For certain types of litigation, we have relationships with co-counsel in other states as well. If you're unsure whether we can help with your specific location, just ask — we'll be straightforward about whether we're the right fit.
What if I can't afford to pay upfront?
For contingency-eligible cases — including most personal injury, wrongful termination, and discrimination claims — you pay nothing until we win. Court costs and case expenses are advanced by the firm and repaid from the settlement. We built our practice this way deliberately, because we believe people shouldn't be priced out of justice. For other case types, we offer flexible payment schedules and will work with you to find a structure that makes sense.
