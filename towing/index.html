Our Story

Tampa Bay's Most Trusted
Towing Family

Ridgeline Towing & Recovery was founded in 2013 by Marcus and Elena Ridgeway — two Tampa locals who believed the towing industry deserved better: better response times, more transparent pricing, and drivers who actually care about the people they help.

Over a decade later, we're proud to operate a growing fleet of modern vehicles, employing certified local operators who treat every vehicle — and every customer — with the highest level of professionalism. We're not a national chain. We're your neighbors, and this community is our home.
